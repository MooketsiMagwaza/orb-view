# Vision

## The feeling

Opening Orb View should feel like entering a clean, living model of a mind.

The screen is mostly white. A small family of blue orbs floats in open space, joined by fine lines that flex as the orbs move. Nothing is boxed into a grid. Nothing asks the user to “choose a path.” The network simply offers nearby thoughts.

Tapping feels exploratory. Pressing and holding feels reflective.

## The experience in motion

At first, Entropy sits near the center with five related ideas arranged loosely around it. Their layout resembles a hexagon but is not rigid. Small differences in drift and blue tone make each thought feel distinct while keeping the system visually unified.

When a node is tapped, the camera eases toward it and its children emerge. Older, less relevant nodes recede rather than disappearing abruptly. The user has moved deeper into the same network, not opened a new page.

When a node is held, it moves to the exact center. Every other orb and connection softens into the white background. A gentle blip marks the change. A colored glow rises near the bottom of the screen and sweeps from side to side as the explanation forms. The finished writing appears beneath the orb as a calm, readable thought.

There is no avatar, speech bubble, input bar, or previous-message list. The concept itself appears to speak.

## Voice

The writing should sound curious, thoughtful, and personal without pretending that an opinion is a fact. Different branches can have different temperaments:

- Entropy is restless and connective.
- Silicon is precise and process-oriented.
- Culture is layered and aware of context.
- Humanity is intimate, curious, and contradictory.
- Nature is quiet and observant.
- Machines are clear, mechanical, and causal.

- The Cosmos is vast and unhurried, and slow to conclude.
- Mathematics is lucid and exact, with nothing wasted.

Those personalities affect rhythm, word choice, blue, and tempo. The orb *form* is chosen separately, for what its motion says about the idea (see [ORBS.md](ORBS.md)).

## Long-term direction

The network can eventually become a personal knowledge space that grows with use:

- concepts can link across branches;
- a person can add or revise their own writing;
- paths can be saved as trails through the mind;
- narration can make the centered concept feel spoken;
- an optional model can generate new connections within curated boundaries;
- the graph can evolve without losing its calm, minimal surface.

The central promise should remain unchanged: explore by tapping, understand by holding.
